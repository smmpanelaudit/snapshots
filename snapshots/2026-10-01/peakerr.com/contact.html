01

How to Contact Support

The recommended support method is to create an account, log in, and submit a ticket through the customer dashboard. Tickets allow our team to verify your account and review order or payment records.

  • Create an account or log in to your existing account.
  • Open the Tickets or Support section.
  • Select the correct support category.
  • Include the order ID, payment ID, or relevant reference.
  • Explain the issue clearly and attach evidence where necessary.
02

Order and Refill Support

For delayed, partial, canceled, incorrect-status, or refill-related orders, provide the exact order ID and a brief explanation.

  • Confirm the target link is still public and unchanged.
  • Check the service description and estimated delivery time.
  • Avoid creating multiple tickets for the same order.
  • Include before-and-after counts when reporting a drop.
03

Payment and Balance Support

For missing deposits, duplicate payments, incorrect balance, charge issues, or refund questions, provide the payment method, amount, date, transaction reference, and supporting screenshot.

Protect sensitive information

Never send your password, one-time password, complete card number, CVV, bank login, wallet private key, or recovery phrase.

04

Support Response Time

Our support team aims to respond as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on ticket volume, issue complexity, provider investigation, payment verification, weekends, and holidays.

Complex order or payment cases may require additional review. Please keep all communication for one issue inside the same ticket whenever possible.

05

Before Contacting Us

Reviewing the following information may help resolve common questions faster:

  • Check the order status and service description.
  • Confirm that the submitted link is correct and public.
  • Review our Terms of Service and Delivery Policy.
  • Review the Refund Policy for balance and payment requests.
  • Check the FAQ and recent service updates.
